### 4. Which comparison the manifest asserts

`internal/fixtures/manifest.go: VerifyAgainstManifest` asserts, in order and reporting *all* problems rather than the first:
1. `runtime.Version()` == manifest `toolchain.go`; `go.mod` `go` directive == manifest `go_mod_language_version`;
2. sha256 over the eleven named generator source files (`generator.source_sha256`), plus generator `Version`, plus that the manifest's `source_files` list still equals `GeneratorSourceFiles`;
3. sha256 of each committed codec input and its recorded `tool_version`;
4. any already-generated corpus in `testdata/fixtures` matches the manifest;
5. then, and only then, regenerate into a scratch dir and compare **sha256 of the bytes**, both directions (fixture missing from manifest, and manifest entry the generator no longer produces).

So the assertion is **bytes**, not decoded pixels — which is what ADR-009 asks for ("a sha256 per file and the generator version"). Decoded-property assertions are a *separate* layer: every fixture's `verifyFn` runs inside `Generate()` itself, so a corpus that hashes but does not exercise its property cannot be written.

If any of steps 1–4 disagree, step 5 is deliberately skipped and a `not-compared` problem is emitted saying the bytes were NOT compared. That is the right direction (it refuses to report a meaningless comparison) — I confirm it does not mask a failure, because the run still exits non-zero.

### 5. Provenance of every byte

**Ten of twelve fixtures are pure Go**, written byte by byte from the standard library by `internal/fixtures/{raster,jpeg,png,gif,webp,mp4}.go`: pixels from a seeded PRNG painter, and the EXIF/GPS IFDs, PNG chunks, GIF blocks, RIFF/VP8L bitstream and ISOBMFF box tree all hand-assembled. No network, no `http`/`https` reference in the generator (the `fixtures` lane greps for this and allows only the SVG namespace string and the `169.254.169.254` written *into* the polyglot as bait — neither is fetched; I confirmed the grep is real and passes).

**Two of twelve are committed binary generator inputs** (`internal/fixtures/codec/avif-still.avif` 1459 B, `internal/fixtures/codec/webm-short.webm` 3564 B), because AVIF needs an AV1 encoder and WebM needs VP8 and no pure-Go encoder exists. Both were encoded from `internal/fixtures/codec/source.png` (7223 B), which the generator produced and which is committed beside them; `TestCommittedCodecSourceIsGeneratorOutput` asserts the committed source.png still equals generator output, so the chain is checked. The exact `vips` and `ffmpeg` argv, tool versions and the TrackUID normalisation are recorded in `fixtures/manifest.json → codec_inputs`. This is disclosed plainly in `NOTICE`, in `internal/fixtures/codec.go` and in the package doc — not papered over. See Finding V-1.

**No third-party material.** Independent check with exiftool 13.30 (alpine 3.22 container I built and removed): **zero ICC profile tags in any of the twelve files** — there is no embedded ICC profile of any provenance in this corpus, so the "where did the ICC profile come from" hazard does not arise. No `Software`, no camera vendor, no `Photoshop`/`GIMP`/`Adobe` marker, no copyright string, no base64 blob in the tree. ADR-009 does not place an ICC fixture in the M0 twelve; its own "M0 obligations" section defers ICC wide-gamut to a later slice, so its absence is correct, not a gap.

`NOTICE` carries a dedicated "Test fixtures (VZ-FOUND-007, ADR-009)" section stating the corpus has no third-party material, naming the two codec-tier exceptions and their tools, explaining the GPS coordinates (Royal Observatory, Greenwich — I confirmed: 51°28'40.12"N 0°0'5.31"W), and recording that no CC0 photograph is present today.

**Size and the load corpus.** Committed corpus size on disk: **0 bytes — the corpus is not committed at all.** `fixtures/manifest.json` (334 lines) is the committed artefact; `.gitignore` excludes `/testdata/fixtures/` and `/testdata/load-corpus/`, with the reason written in the file: a committed copy would make "the generator reproduces it byte-identically" untestable. ## Verdict

# PASS

**VZ-FOUND-007's acceptance bullet is MET by my own evidence.**

> "the pinned generator reproduces the twelve M0 fixtures byte-identically against the committed sha256 manifest, and a changed fixture fails the manifest check (demonstrated)"

- **Twelve fixtures, byte-identically**: I hashed the corpus the ubuntu-24.04/amd64 acceptance-platform runner produced for this SHA and every one of the twelve matched the committed manifest, with no extra files; I separately regenerated on darwin/arm64 and got the same twelve; and the `fixtures` lane log shows CI *regenerated and compared* rather than hashing committed files (the corpus is not committed at all).
- **Deterministic**: identical across seven variations of TZ, LANG, umask, `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH` and working directory/path, and across two architectures. No timestamp, path, username or tool-version string leaks into any fixture.
- **A changed fixture fails the check**: reproduced by me with a one-byte flip, red and naming the file — plus a changed generator, three classes of moved pin, and the floor deletion, each red by name and each restored to green.
- **The twelve really exercise their properties**: verified with exiftool 13.30, `file` 5.46, libvips 8.18.2, ffprobe and my own Go decode probe — tools independent of the builder's tests. Orientation 6 is really 6; GPS is really there; the truncated JPEG really has no EOI and really fails to decode in 0 ms and 0 MiB; the 900 MP fixture really declares 30000×30000 in 122 KB and really allocates 858 MiB if decoded unguarded; the bomb really inflates ~1012×; the polyglot really sniffs as `image/gif` under a `.svg` name; the animations really have distinct frames; the MP4 really demuxes to twelve decodable JPEGs; the AVIF really decodes, with a decoder ADR-001 puts in the default image.
- **Provenance**: no third-party photograph, no downloaded image, no embedded ICC profile of any origin, no base64 blob, no network access at build or test time. The three committed binaries total 12 246 bytes and all trace to the generator's own `source.png`. `NOTICE` states the licence position.
- **CI**: all six required lanes executed and are green on `56920fe`, `ci-required` collected all six and succeeded, and both the manifest and the guard's floor list `fixtures`. GitGuardian green; my own credential scan of the diff found nothing.
- **No test weakened or deleted**; no migration or `api/` byte moved; the gate list and floor both grew.

The four findings are all SHOULD or NIT, none blocking. V-1 is an ADR-009 amendment owed by the meta repo, which the builder correctly declined to make itself. V-2 and V-3 are small hardening improvements to `fixtures-verify`'s own reporting, already covered in CI by the test suite the same lane runs.

**PASS is not a merge and not VERIFIED.** The chair records those; the head must not have moved (it had not at `56920fec76c51f790de4506cef1fe79287d1cd12` when I finished).
