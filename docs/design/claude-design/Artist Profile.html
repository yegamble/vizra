Alex Rivera

@alex.rivera · Urban Brutalism
Mexico City · Berlin Member since 2019 alexrivera.studio

Alex Rivera is a documentary and architectural photographer working between Mexico City and Berlin. His long-form studies of post‑war concrete — bus stations, housing blocks, civic theatres — treat the material as a slow-moving, almost liturgical subject.

His work has appeared in Aperture, The Plan, and the permanent collection at MoMA Mexico. Currently working on Concrete Sundays, a twelve‑city series due early 2027.
